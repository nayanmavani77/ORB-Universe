What the numbers say

The book is one-sided.

Short trades net 66.4k USD while long trades net -16.5k USD. Every unit of profit comes from one direction, so this is a directional bet on the period rather than a symmetric breakout.

The result rests on a few large winners.

Trades of 2R or better are 128 of 822 (16%) but contribute MORE than the whole net profit (995%) — everything else nets out negative. Miss a handful of them and the edge largely disappears.

Only the first breakout of a session pays.

Trade #1 averages 287 USD; every later trade in the same session averages -93 USD. Setting max_trades_per_session to 1 is worth testing.

The edge lives in wider opening ranges.

The narrowest fifth averages -24 USD per trade and the widest fifth 428 USD. A minimum range-width filter would remove the losing half without touching the strategy rules.

At least one trade was held through a closed market.

The longest hold was 55.8 hours, which spans a weekend or a session break. That is gap risk the stop time was meant to prevent — check the journal for a LATE stop-time warning.
