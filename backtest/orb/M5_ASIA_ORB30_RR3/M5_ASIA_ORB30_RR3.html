What the numbers say

The result rests on a few large winners.

Trades of 2R or better are 55 of 199 (28%) but contribute MORE than the whole net profit (250%) — everything else nets out negative. Miss a handful of them and the edge largely disappears.

The edge lives in wider opening ranges.

The narrowest fifth averages -130 USD per trade and the widest fifth 1,048 USD. A minimum range-width filter would remove the losing half without touching the strategy rules.
